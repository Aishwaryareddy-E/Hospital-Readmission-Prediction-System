POTENTIAL QUESTIONS & ANSWERS:

Q: Why did you choose these specific features?
A: Features were selected based on domain knowledge and their known correlation
   with hospital readmissions in healthcare literature.

Q: How did you handle class imbalance?
A: Used SMOTE (Synthetic Minority Over-sampling Technique) to create synthetic
   samples of the minority class, achieving balanced class distribution.

Q: Which model performed best and why?
A: Logistic Regression achieved the highest ROC-AUC (0.6450). While tree-based
   ensemble methods typically perform well, the linear relationships in this
   dataset were well-captured by logistic regression.

Q: What are the limitations?
A: The model could be improved with hyperparameter tuning, additional feature
   engineering, and larger datasets. Also, external validation on different
   populations would strengthen generalizability.

Q: How can this be deployed in practice?
A: The system includes a FastAPI component that can be integrated with hospital
   EHR systems to make real-time predictions at discharge time.
